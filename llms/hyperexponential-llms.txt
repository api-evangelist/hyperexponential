# hyperexponential

> hyperexponential (hx) is a London-based insurtech building the #1 pricing and underwriting decision platform for commercial property & casualty (P&C) insurance. Actuaries and underwriters use its Hyperoperator assistant, no-code Workflow Builder, Calculation Engines, and Portfolio Intelligence to build, deploy, and iterate pricing models and underwriting workflows. Top global carriers run $75bn+ in annual commercial P&C premium on the platform. Backed by a16z and Battery Ventures.
